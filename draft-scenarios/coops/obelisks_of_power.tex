\addscenariosection{1}{Cooperative Scenario}{Obelisks of power}{\images/obeliskOfPower.png}

\begin{multicols}{2}

\textbf{Author:} Kelghor

\textit{Dark Wizard Abemonapophix was awaken in his Dragon Utopia. Only brave hereoes can stop him to destroy powerfull obelisks. Its nessesary to meet with our allies before gates of his fort and destroy him. Maybye tha obelisks can help us to defeat him, but they are guarded by powerful monsters, so be careful when you want to use them.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

This Scenario is played over 9 Rounds.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 1--6

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 30 \svg{gold}, 6 \svg{building_materials}, 2 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 15 \svg{gold}, 2 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 3 × A ``Few'' \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze} Dwelling, Citadel, Mage Guild (cant be applied build bonus)

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout ($P$ stands for the number of players):

\begin{itemize}
  \item P × Starting (I) Map Tile
  \item P × Far (II--III)
  \item 2P × Near (IV--V) P * Map Tiles are with obelisks and they are adjacent to the Center Map Tile.
  \item 1 × Center (VI--VII)
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}

\begin{itemize}
   \item Win final fight against the Dark Wizard (see section below).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Defeat Conditions}}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Any player loses their Hero.
  \item Lose final fight.
  \item Any hero is not on Dragos Utopia at the end of \nth{9} Round.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}
\textbf{\nth{1} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item At the start of the Round, all players heroes lvl up to level III.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{3} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove all Black Cubes from Water Wheels, Windmills and Leprechauns on the map.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{5} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Explore your Map tile from pool, for free anywhere on map.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{6} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove all Black Cubes from Water Wheels, Windmills and Leprechauns on the map.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{\nth{9} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Remove all Black Cubes from Water Wheels, Windmills and Leprechauns on the map.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Players can spend their Build Token at any time to perform Trading Post actions.
  \item If two allied Heroes are on adjacent Fields, they can freely trade resources, Units and Artifacts.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Visiting an Obelisk}}
The battle under obelisk of power is special fight, where you fight with level of your choise, dependt on level it rewadrs you.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Choose army level freely from IV-VII.
  \item You cant escape from this fight, so you have to win it.
  \item If you win, choose one of:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Gain [Army level] * 3 \svg{gold}
    \item Buy one of defeated Units for half price (rounded down) -  This army can't be used for common fights on the map, BUT can be brought to the final fight againts Dark Wizard.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Final fight with Dark Wizard}}
Dark Wizard waits for you in Dragons Utopia, only all players together can fight him, so you have to cooperate to win.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Setup army of Dark Wizard by table below, based on number of players and difficulty level.
  \item The number of enemy Units on battlefield is limited up to 8 Units.
  \item In Fight against Dark Wizard, all players fight together against him in one battle.
  \item Each player can bring up only one Unit to the fight.
  \item Each player can bring one Unit from the Obelisk, but it doesn't have to be the same Unit as the one they fought with.
  \item All players fight simoutaneously, so they all can use their cards of abilities, artifacts and spells but only one player can stack bonuses for one action. (for emaxmple two players cant boost same attack by card)
  \item Morale token can be played anytime.
  \item This combat does not cost \svg{movement}.
  \item At start of fight, the Dark Wizard spells "Silencio!", so nobody can speak about stragegy during the fight, but players can still communicate about their actions and rules.
\end{itemize}
